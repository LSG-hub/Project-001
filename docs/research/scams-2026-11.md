Researched 2026-11-21. Some figures come from press reports of official data; these are marked "(press)". Indian totals depend on the measure used (all cybercrime complaints, the "cheating" subset, or financial fraud on NCRP), so always name the measure when quoting.

## Scam types the assistant must recognise

| Scam | How the call goes | Red-flag phrases | The ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| Digital arrest (fake CBI, ED, NCB, customs, TRAI, courier) | Parcel with drugs or Aadhaar "misuse", then a transfer to fake police, then a video call, secrecy, and a "verification" transfer | "Aap digital arrest mein hain", "kisi ko mat batana", "camera on rakho", "Supreme Court order" | Large transfers, breaking FDs, Aadhaar, PAN |
| DoT/TRAI SIM cut-off | "Number band ho jayega in 2 hours", often a WhatsApp call from +92 | "illegal activity on your SIM" | Personal data, then hand-off to digital arrest |
| Bank / KYC / card block | Fake bank executive, link or app | "KYC update nahi hua", "OTP bata dijiye" | OTP, PIN, CVV, app install |
| Electricity cut-off | "Bijli aaj raat kat jayegi" | "bill update nahi hua" | Small fee, APK, screen share |
| UPI "sent by mistake" / QR | "Galti se paise bhej diye", "scan to receive" | "PIN daalo receive karne ke liye" | QR scan or PIN entry that actually pays them |
| Army officer / OLX | Fake army ID, QR "to pay you" | "Main army mein hoon" | QR scan, advance payment |
| Relative in trouble / voice clone | "Aapka beta pakda gaya hai", crying voice, call cut before you can check | "abhi settle kar lo" | Immediate UPI transfer |
| Sextortion video call | Explicit video call is recorded, then a fake police threat | "video viral kar denge" | Repeated payments |
| Investment / tasks / jobs | Starts in WhatsApp or Telegram groups; calls push for more | "guaranteed returns", "withdrawal ke liye tax bharo" | Deposits |
| Loan-app recovery | Abuse, morphed photos sent to contacts | "photo sabko bhej denge" | Payments well beyond the loan |
| SIM swap / eSIM | "Upgrade to eSIM", link or OTP | "OTP aayega, bata dena" | OTP |
| Call-forwarding hijack | "Dial this code" | "yeh code dial kariye" | Forwards your OTPs to them |
| Fake customer care / AnyDesk | A searched helpline is answered by a mobile number | "AnyDesk download karo", "₹10 refund" | Remote access |
| Insurance bonus, lottery, KBC, PM-Kisan APK | Fee to release money | "processing fee", "app install karo" | Fee, APK |

## Detection signals, strongest first
1. **A request for a credential or action:** OTP, PIN, CVV, Aadhaar or PAN, installing an app or APK, screen sharing, dialling a code, scanning a QR "to receive", moving money to a "safe account", or paying a fee to release money. Close to decisive on its own.
2. **Authority plus threat:** police, CBI, ED, TRAI, DoT, RBI or a court, combined with arrest, FIR, disconnection or a freeze.
3. **Secrecy and isolation:** "don't tell family", "stay on video", "don't cut the call".
4. **Manufactured urgency:** "within 2 hours", "tonight".
5. **A move to video or WhatsApp.** Officials never arrest anyone over video.
6. **Origin:** foreign or WhatsApp numbers (+92 is named by DoT; +855, +95, +856 and +66 are our inference from where scam compounds operate), or "officials" calling from 10-digit mobile numbers.
7. **Hand-offs:** "transferring you to the officer", a second voice, call-centre noise.
8. **A distressed relative plus a payment demand.** Treat the voice itself as unverifiable.
9. **Number reputation:** DoT's Financial Fraud Risk Indicator, and CNAP names.

**False-positive traps:**
- **Real banks** call from 1600xx numbers. TRAI does not permit blocking or filtering 1600 calls, so get a legal read. Judge what is being asked for, not the caller ID.
- **Real courier OTPs** at the door are normal. The danger is a caller asking for an OTP the user never triggered.
- **Real police** don't demand money, video custody or secrecy.
- **Relatives on new numbers, doctors and hospitals:** never just hang up. Take a message and alert the family.

## Assistant behaviour
**Do:**
- Answer in the caller's language.
- Say it's an AI and that the call may be recorded.
- Collect the caller's name, organisation, reference number and callback number.
- Stall politely.
- Tell the family to verify through the official number on the bank's website or card, or the relative's saved number.
- If money moved, tell them to **call 1930 at once** (the golden hour) or file at cybercrime.gov.in. Otherwise, report through Chakshu on Sanchar Saathi.
- Prepare a report packet: number, time, transcript, any UPI or account IDs.

**Never:**
- Share or confirm an OTP, PIN, account, Aadhaar or PAN, address, family details or schedules.
- Confirm where family members are.
- Agree to install, dial, scan, pay or stay on the line.
- Put a suspected scammer through to the elder.
- Insult the caller, impersonate police, or call a number "safe" because its caller ID looks right.

## Family alert triggers
- **Instant push plus a phone call to the family member:**
  - any credential, app, QR or payment request;
  - a digital-arrest or authority-plus-threat script;
  - a "relative in trouble" call;
  - the elder picks up or calls back a flagged number;
  - a call longer than 10 minutes with a flagged number.
- **Soft alert:** repeated calls from the same unknown number, foreign or WhatsApp calls, or a burst of flagged calls (the elder may be on a target list).
- **Daily digest** of everything screened.
- **After a high-risk alert,** prompt: "Did any money move? Call 1930 now."

## Legal notes (needs Indian counsel)
- **Recording:** India has no specific call-recording law. A party recording their own call is generally not a crime, but there is privacy risk under Article 21. The assistant acts as the user's agent; announce recording anyway.
- **DPDP:** the Act and Rules were notified Nov 2025, and most obligations apply from about 13 May 2027. The caller is also a data principal, so give notice up front, minimise data, retain it briefly, and store it in India and encrypted.
- **Auto-reporting:** no public NCRP or Chakshu API was found. Talk to I4C and DoT.
- **Partnership idea:** India has no bank "trusted contact" scheme. The US (FINRA 4512) and UK have one.

## For the product
- Carrier forwarding can't select "unknown numbers only".
  - On basic phones the assistant gets unanswered or declined calls (or all calls, with a whitelist pass-through on our side).
  - On smartphones our app can decline unknown callers automatically.
- Scammers abuse forwarding codes, so our onboarding must look nothing like a scam.
